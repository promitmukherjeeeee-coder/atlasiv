**Additions, 24 Nov 2026 (owner-confirmed interface v3.3):**

- **ADR-016 Interface ID convention and Interface Architecture Registry.** Permanent ID families LV, CG-, WS-, /V, DS-, AN-, GS-, AP-, XL-, held in a machine-readable registry with the chart as its visual companion. Alternative considered: names only. Reason: stable references for specs, tests and saves. Status: owner-confirmed.
- **ADR-017 Interface levels use LV1 to LV4.** The chart's L1 to L4 collides with mechanics L1 to L10 (Living Constitutional Order). Status: delegated; the chart label can stay L1 to L4 visually, while the registry and docs use LV.
- **ADR-018 The interface registry references data ownership.** Ownership is defined only in the World State and Data Schema (document B). Reason: one source of truth. Status: delegated.

**Additions, 24 Nov 2026 (knowledge and historiography amendment, section 59).** Status "owner-confirmed" means the owner's amendment sets the requirement; anything marked "proposed" needs engineering evaluation before it becomes a final implementation decision. Dependencies name mechanics (Mechanics section Q) and passes.

- **ADR-019 Universal Emergent Causality.** Every gameplay outcome, including the pressure toward FTL, arises from simulated conditions through declared dependencies; no scripted bonus triggered by era, title or technology name (no Internet research bonus, no era-forced industrialisation). Alternatives: scripted incentives (previous X5 wording). Consequences: X5 rewritten; every mechanic spec must name its causal inputs; counterfactual tests required. Dependencies: Pass 2b, Causal Inspector. Status: owner-confirmed. Amended 24 Nov 2026: one declared exogenous input, the FTL horizon value (ADR-036).
- **ADR-020 Knowledge–Capability Separation.** Ownership, custody, access, acquisition, comprehension, absorption, operational capability and deployment are distinct states (K43, K44, K48, K50); a technology is never a Boolean unlock. Alternatives: classic tech tree unlocks. Consequences: K1 amended; capability per actor, region, institution and industry; tacit, codified, embodied, institutional, distributed, operational and dormant forms (K49); discipline-specific absorptive capacity. Status: owner-confirmed; state representation proposed for Pass 2.
- **ADR-021 Separation of objective truth, observation, actor knowledge, research, interpretation and publication.** Objective history is owned by the event store (X10); everything else derives from it and never feeds back into it except through authorised systems (X24, K61). Research never reads hidden variables. Extends ADR-015. Status: owner-confirmed.
- **ADR-022 Generalised knowledge artifact identity, content separated from copies.** One entity type (K40) with sparse components and permanent IDs; non-rivalrous content separate from rivalrous carriers, editions and translations (K42); shared content with provenance; significant works individual, ordinary output aggregated. Alternatives: per-holder book records. Status: requirement owner-confirmed; storage layout proposed, decided in Pass 2.
- **ADR-023 Source independence.** Copies, translations, summaries, repetition and circular citation never count as independent evidence (K54); represented as a sparse provenance and dependency graph. Status: owner-confirmed; graph algorithm proposed.
- **ADR-024 Capability, deployment, regression and restoration.** Capability states and transitions (K50) consume real production resources; regression when dependencies decay; codified knowledge can survive capability loss; restoration uses surviving knowledge, expertise and infrastructure (K51). Candidate mathematics: hazards with time-to-event sampling (ADR-012). Status: requirement owner-confirmed; formulation proposed.
- **ADR-025 Era-independent transmission and open publication.** One set of transmission media properties for all eras (K46); open-network publication resolved by publication scopes and lazy queries, not per-actor copies or bilateral treaties (K47). Status: owner-confirmed; scope algorithm proposed.
- **ADR-026 Institutional collections and recursive synthesis.** Institutions hold collections with capabilities and survive succession (K52); synthesis produces new attributable artifacts usable as later sources; quality from coverage, independence, reliability, competence and hard prerequisites, never from counts (K53). Status: owner-confirmed; production function proposed.
- **ADR-027 Theories, peer review, consensus and Grand Intellectual Works.** Theories can be wrong; review is fallible; replication is distinct from acceptance; scientific consensus is distinct from political, public, religious and commercial acceptance (K55, K56); Grand Intellectual Works are emergent classifications that can compete and be superseded (K57); intellectual genealogy tracked without rewarding volume (K60). Status: owner-confirmed.
- **ADR-028 Historically situated access, persistent editions, on-demand compilation.** No future knowledge in past works or decisions; editions dated and preserved after later research or cache invalidation; publications compiled on demand from records (X21, X25). Status: owner-confirmed.
- **ADR-029 Rendering separated from simulation; offline baseline; optional language-model renderers.** A deterministic offline renderer is always available; optional language-model renderers are replaceable, validated against claims, fall back on failure, and never change state hashes (X25). Alternatives: required online model. Status: owner-confirmed; renderer selection proposed for Pass 10.
- **ADR-030 Access modes and archive controls.** Normal, authorised perspective, debug tag-switch and omniscient developer modes (A14), no leakage of debug knowledge into actor knowledge; archive access controlled by rights (K43). Status: owner-confirmed.
- **ADR-031 Export interoperability.** Open formats without DRM, respecting access modes (X27). Status: requirement owner-confirmed; formats proposed for Pass 10.
- **ADR-032 Extension compatibility.** No modding support, no plugin or extension API and no mod tools; only the source code is published. Everything the amendments ask for (campaign histories, renderers, readers, export) is built in-house as capability expansions. Every published work and edition records the ruleset version and build identifier. Alternatives: plugin API; modded-campaign support. Reason: owner decision; mods are not needed to deliver the requested capabilities. Status: owner-confirmed 24 Nov 2026.
- **ADR-033 Computational scaling and aggregation for knowledge and history.** Deduplicated content, shared scopes, sparse access and dependency graphs, lazy queries, candidate filtering, event-driven invalidation and cached rendering (A15); measured with knowledge-environment benchmarks (Computational Doctrine section 19). Status: proposed, confirm at Gate 1 and Pass 7a gates.
- **ADR-034 Cross-domain causal traceability.** Every authoritative change records its declared cause so the Causal Inspector can name direct and indirect contributors; benefits only through authorised systems with declared owner, scope, conditions and duration, no duplicated benefits (K61). Status: owner-confirmed; record format decided in Pass 2.
- **ADR-035 Truth–Evidence–Belief–Consequence Separation.** Truth, evidence, evidentiary strength, credibility, belief, institutional and public acceptance, strategic utility and consequences are separate quantities (K63, AX-8); information leverage arises from expected consequences, not truth (D22); decisions use beliefs and expectations of others (D23); consequences persist after correction (K65). Alternatives: truth-weighted effects, where false information is automatically weaker. Reason: owner axiom; matches how propaganda, scandals, panics and forged pretexts work. Dependencies: ADR-015, ADR-021, ADR-023, ADR-034. Status: requirement owner-confirmed; credibility and leverage formulations proposed for Pass 7a.
- **ADR-036 FTL horizon value (X28).** The real benefits of FTL lie beyond Earth and after the game ends, where no simulated system can produce them, so they enter as one hard-coded, declared incentive known to every actor. It feeds only valuation terms (AI and institutional utility for FTL-related research, institutions, funding and sponsorship; sponsor-share value; the FTL contribution score) and never grants progress, capability or project stages. Alternatives: purely emergent pressure (cannot represent benefits outside the simulation); scripted bonuses to research (would break AX-1 and AX-2). Consequences: AX-1 names X28 as its only exogenous input; tests KH-111 and KH-112. Status: owner-confirmed 24 Nov 2026; size and time profile are calibration parameters. Amended 25 Nov 2026: the value is public and identical, but its weight per actor scales with credible awareness, feasible research paths and expected consequences of investment, so it does not dominate prehistory; FTL stays speculative physics and is not guaranteed (test RC-11).
- **ADR-037 Registries as versioned YAML files in the repository.** interface\_registry.yaml, mechanic\_registry.yaml, state\_ownership.yaml and system\_dependency\_map.yaml live in the repository, are validated by schema, and generate the interface chart and the dependency checks. Alternatives: registries kept only in the documents; a database. Reason: diffable, reviewable, readable by code and by Claude Code. Status: delegated; draft 0.1.0 created 24 Nov 2026, committed in Pass 2.
- **ADR-038 Twenty-eight owner systems with single-writer ownership.** Every authoritative variable has exactly one owner system; other systems change it only through declared events, orders or ledger transactions; knowledge (OS-KNOW), actor belief (OS-INFO) and capability (OS-PROD) are separate owners. Alternatives: per-mechanic owners; shared mutable state. Reason: determinism, conservation and causal tracing. Consequences: the system-level dependency draft shows one coupled core of 19 systems once phase-lagged edges are removed (17 before the 26 Nov urban links), so Pass 2b must assign each cross-system link a timing (same stage, next phase or turn end) and Pass 3 a solver contract where a loop closes inside one update. Status: proposed, confirmed in Pass 2.
- **ADR-039 Campaign manifest.** Every campaign records core build identifier, ruleset version, schema version, active first-party expansions with exact versions and load order, content hashes, execution configuration, world seed and initial conditions; replay requires an identical manifest. Alternatives: seed and ruleset only. Reason: identical configuration and inputs must give identical histories, and different builds must not be expected to. There are no third-party mods; modified source gets its own build identifier. Status: owner-confirmed 25 Nov 2026; built in Pass 1 J8.
- **ADR-040 Observer-independent cadence.** Update cadence depends only on simulation state, never on the player's view, selection or controlled country. Alternatives: player-proximity level of detail (previous A2). Reason: watching or switching country must not change outcomes. Consequences: A2 and Vision 8 and 9.9 revised; test RC-05 in Pass 1 J9. Status: owner-confirmed 25 Nov 2026.
- **ADR-041 Substep budget.** Four adaptive substeps is the initial engineering budget, not an accuracy ceiling; escalation is deterministic and logged. Alternatives: hard cap of 4. Reason: unstable systems need accuracy, and benchmarks will show the real cost. Status: owner-confirmed 25 Nov 2026; escalation rule proposed in Pass 3.
- **ADR-042 Money representation tests.** 64-bit minor units with 128-bit aggregation, tested against denomination change, high inflation, redenomination and aggregate exposure; a per-currency scale is added in Pass 2 only if the tests require it. Status: proposed; tests in Pass 1 J1, decision in Pass 2.
- **ADR-043 Nominal and real separation.** AX-9, E36 to E38: financial claims and real stocks have separate ledgers; credit is double-entry across its whole lifecycle; international financial institutions are institutions with balance sheets and mandates. Alternatives: money as a generic resource; world bank as a bonus source. Reason: owner requirement; prevents printing money into goods. Status: owner-confirmed 25 Nov 2026; formulations in Pass 7b.
- **ADR-044 Hierarchical decision architecture and delegation.** No single general-purpose AI: objectives, institutional priorities, mandates, specialised decision systems and individuals in roles, each with its own interests, information, authority and horizon (A13); the player uses the same mandate and delegation layer (A16). Alternatives: one central AI; mandatory micromanagement. Reason: competent actors at every level and manageable player workload. Status: owner-confirmed 25 Nov 2026; designed in Pass 5.
- **ADR-045 Settlement-driven subdivision and the one-city rule.** Provinces are carved when a promoted town or city's catchment meets deterministic eligibility; one city per administrative region; a second city needs lawful reorganisation meeting geographic and functional eligibility, with cost escalation; nothing is duplicated. Alternatives: city tied to a fixed region with no subdivision (25 Nov text); free subdivision. Reason: owner direction 26 Nov reconciles growth-driven territory with the exploit concern. Status: requirement owner-confirmed; thresholds proposed, calibrated later.
- **ADR-046 Universal construction lifecycle and actor participation.** Every actor uses one land, project and construction lifecycle (R31) with real inputs; institutions act independently within charters and rights. Alternatives: lightweight institutional output. Status: owner-confirmed 26 Nov 2026.
- **ADR-047 Rights relationship model.** Ownership, lease, concession, access, use, development, operation, administration, taxation, sovereignty, influence and control are separate relations (I19) held by OS-LEDGER. Alternatives: single owner field. Status: requirement owner-confirmed; schema decided in Pass 2.
- **ADR-048 Proposal allocation and site ranking.** Bounded deterministic evaluation on one planning snapshot with stable tie-breaks; candidate algorithms compared in Pass 5. Status: proposed.
- **ADR-049 Aggregated urban morphology with promotion to individual assets.** Ordinary fabric aggregated; significant facilities, projects and rights individual; promotion only on functional need, with conservation. Alternatives: per-building agents. Reason: computational doctrine. Status: proposed, confirm with Gate 1 and Pass 4 benchmarks.
- **ADR-050 Display projection separate from simulation geometry.** One canonical geometry; an invertible display transform toward the owner's 43,200 by 25,200 (12:7) target; candidates compared before selection; tiled GPU rendering with LOD. Status: requirement owner-confirmed; projection choice open, decided before Pass 10.
- **ADR-051 Fixed typed adjacency beside network benefits.** Adjacency (P8) is fixed and typed: one bonus per qualifying neighbour type, several types per district, non-stacking within a type, additive across types, never compounding; the +3 city and +1 town values are the settlement type. Network and distance benefits (R33) are a separate channel, and a pair of places counts through one channel only, so nothing is counted twice. Alternatives: accessibility only, with the old values as calibration (25 Nov wording, superseded); fixed adjacency that grows with neighbour count. Status: owner-confirmed 26 Nov 2026; type catalogue and values calibrated in Pass 4 and Pass 5.
- **ADR-052 Living City interface placement.** WS-A1/V1 Urban Development and City Administration as a full-screen showcase view inside WS-A1, with existing dossier and analysis types. Alternatives: a new workspace or menu item (forbidden by the locked architecture). Status: owner-confirmed 26 Nov 2026; ID checked free.
- **ADR-053 Wonders of the World designated per era.** Great works are built; at the end of each of four world eras seven are designated wonders automatically, 28 per campaign, by a deterministic score (grandeur, cost and uniqueness set by the owner; technical ambition and renown proposed), recorded in the historiography and the Heritage score with no simulated bonus. Era windows are fixed dates (10,000 to 3000 BCE, 3000 BCE to 500 CE, 500 to 1500 CE, 1500 CE to campaign end), so no actor can move a boundary and eras still never act as causes. Alternatives: era boundaries from the world's aggregate condition (gameable and threshold-heavy); a title that grants prestige (would need a declared causal path). Status: requirement owner-confirmed 26 Nov 2026; windows, weights and shortfall rule proposed.
- **ADR-054 Population known only through counts.** Every population figure an actor sees, the player's own included, comes from a census, register or estimate with coverage, accuracy and date; none equals the truth. Supersedes the Vision statement that the player sees exact own statistics. Alternatives: exact own statistics (previous wording). Status: owner-confirmed 26 Nov 2026; error model proposed.
- **ADR-055 Administrative units below the province as jurisdiction relations.** Municipalities, wards, boroughs, village councils and special areas are administration relations over geography at any level, separate from physical districts. Alternatives: fixed administrative tiers; reusing districts as administrative units (mixes physical and legal). Status: requirement owner-confirmed 26 Nov 2026; schema in Pass 2.
- **ADR-056 Divided polity with per-domain control and common components.** A coup or civil war leaves one entity with claimant factions; control is resolved per domain and per place; uncaptured components stay common to all factions; the player leads one faction. Alternatives: an immediate split into two entities (loses the in-between state); a single owner field (cannot express divided control). Status: requirement owner-confirmed 26 Nov 2026; resolution rules proposed in Pass 5.
