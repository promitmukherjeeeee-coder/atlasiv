# ADR-019: Universal Emergent Causality

Group in the original registry: Additions, 24 Nov 2026 (knowledge and historiography amendment, section 59). See [README.md](README.md) for the group note.

Every gameplay outcome, including the pressure toward FTL, arises from simulated conditions through declared dependencies; no scripted bonus triggered by era, title or technology name (no Internet research bonus, no era-forced industrialisation). Alternatives: scripted incentives (previous X5 wording). Consequences: X5 rewritten; every mechanic spec must name its causal inputs; counterfactual tests required. Dependencies: Pass 2b, Causal Inspector. Status: owner-confirmed. Amended 24 Nov 2026: one declared exogenous input, the FTL horizon value (ADR-036).
