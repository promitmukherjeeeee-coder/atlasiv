# ADR-035: Truth–Evidence–Belief–Consequence Separation

Group in the original registry: Additions, 24 Nov 2026 (knowledge and historiography amendment, section 59). See [README.md](README.md) for the group note.

Truth, evidence, evidentiary strength, credibility, belief, institutional and public acceptance, strategic utility and consequences are separate quantities (K63, AX-8); information leverage arises from expected consequences, not truth (D22); decisions use beliefs and expectations of others (D23); consequences persist after correction (K65). Alternatives: truth-weighted effects, where false information is automatically weaker. Reason: owner axiom; matches how propaganda, scandals, panics and forged pretexts work. Dependencies: ADR-015, ADR-021, ADR-023, ADR-034. Status: requirement owner-confirmed; credibility and leverage formulations proposed for Pass 7a.
