# ADR-026: Institutional collections and recursive synthesis

Group in the original registry: Additions, 24 Nov 2026 (knowledge and historiography amendment, section 59). See [README.md](README.md) for the group note.

Institutions hold collections with capabilities and survive succession (K52); synthesis produces new attributable artifacts usable as later sources; quality from coverage, independence, reliability, competence and hard prerequisites, never from counts (K53). Status: owner-confirmed; production function proposed.
