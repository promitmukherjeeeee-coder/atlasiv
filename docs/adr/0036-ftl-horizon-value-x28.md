# ADR-036: FTL horizon value (X28)

Group in the original registry: Additions, 24 Nov 2026 (knowledge and historiography amendment, section 59). See [README.md](README.md) for the group note.

The real benefits of FTL lie beyond Earth and after the game ends, where no simulated system can produce them, so they enter as one hard-coded, declared incentive known to every actor. It feeds only valuation terms (AI and institutional utility for FTL-related research, institutions, funding and sponsorship; sponsor-share value; the FTL contribution score) and never grants progress, capability or project stages. Alternatives: purely emergent pressure (cannot represent benefits outside the simulation); scripted bonuses to research (would break AX-1 and AX-2). Consequences: AX-1 names X28 as its only exogenous input; tests KH-111 and KH-112. Status: owner-confirmed 24 Nov 2026; size and time profile are calibration parameters.
