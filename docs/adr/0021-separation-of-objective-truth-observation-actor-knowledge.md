# ADR-021: Separation of objective truth, observation, actor knowledge, research, interpretation and publication

Group in the original registry: Additions, 24 Nov 2026 (knowledge and historiography amendment, section 59). See [README.md](README.md) for the group note.

Objective history is owned by the event store (X10); everything else derives from it and never feeds back into it except through authorised systems (X24, K61). Research never reads hidden variables. Extends ADR-015. Status: owner-confirmed.
