# ADR-027: Theories, peer review, consensus and Grand Intellectual Works

Group in the original registry: Additions, 24 Nov 2026 (knowledge and historiography amendment, section 59). See [README.md](README.md) for the group note.

Theories can be wrong; review is fallible; replication is distinct from acceptance; scientific consensus is distinct from political, public, religious and commercial acceptance (K55, K56); Grand Intellectual Works are emergent classifications that can compete and be superseded (K57); intellectual genealogy tracked without rewarding volume (K60).
